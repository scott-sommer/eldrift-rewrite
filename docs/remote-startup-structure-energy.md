Passive physical settlement and ambient cash are now joined in standalone spawn timelines; passive household/project integration and matching native worker calibration remain open. Sections below retain the incremental evidence and compatibility history.

The aggregate spawn pool cannot justify a transfer into one structure using another structure's free capacity. This ledger tracks each declared spawn/extension separately, with outgoing-stock and incoming-capacity reservations as liabilities. A birth debit follows the supplied origin order atomically; insufficient stock or a debit touching reserved stock leaves the ledger unchanged. It does not rearrange native debits to evade reservations. Deposits stop at the chosen structure's unreserved capacity. Births precede transfers within a modeled frame.

The caller supplies the exact charge order. Existing native spawn execution omits energyStructures; pinned engine defaults to owned active spawns sorted by distance, then extensions sorted by distance. Explicit lists use their declared order. The ledger does not infer distance ties or certify that a future native producer supplies the same order.

Checkpoint format 1 binds initial physical facts, reservations and orders. Restore replays bounded events and checks point conservation; structure enumeration order is immaterial, charge order is material. Limits: 128 structures, 16 origins, 8192 events, 1 MiB checkpoint. The ledger owns neither production Memory nor real reservations, actions, queues or cash receipts.

## Ambient cash attribution

The standalone startup cash ledger now has an explicit third-argument option { ambient: true }. Only that mode accepts a sixth frame argument for already-settled ambient energy and emits an ambientEnergy counter (including zero). Ambient credits settle after frame expenses/minimum-cash measurement, so they cannot retroactively finance that frame's birth. They increase actual ending cash and can reduce a later cash shortfall, but do not increase deliveredEnergy or firstDeliveryAt. Surplus and construction recovery subtract cumulative ambientEnergy, so a native gift cannot make the source appear to repay birth/construction costs. Shared cash balance arithmetic and extracted recovery settlement keep complexity bounded.

Checkpoint restore requires the ambient counter only in ambient mode, reconciles ending cash including ambient input, and reconciles source surplus excluding it. Default mode rejects ambient credit before mutation and retains its original checkpoint/result shape. Legacy readers refuse ambient checkpoints through their strict counter-field checks; this standalone option is not yet enabled by any project constructor. Physical regeneration settlement, links from ambient cash totals to physical events, and compatible project/planning versions remain required. A statistical ledger alone cannot authenticate the physical source of credits.

## Standalone passive funding integration

Passive spawn timelines now settle the physical ledger after household action callbacks and before cash settlement. The exact returned ambient amount credits bank/accessibility together and enters the opt-in cash ledger; it does not increase refilledEnergy or delivery income. Admission runs before that credit, so an initially empty spawn can pay a50-energy body only at frame50 after50 earlier gifts, not during an earlier frame. The final frame can regenerate again. Restore binds ambientThrough to tick-1, authenticates the cash ambient counter against re-derived physical history, restores last-frame attribution, and checks that payable cash never went negative.

In passive mode funding.capacity is physical capacity minus outgoing reserved stock. Incoming capacity claims still constrain each manual deposit, but do not reduce the attainable stored-energy ceiling because automatic native input can occupy them. Non-passive capacity semantics remain unchanged. Physical transfers and cargo losses now bound outside cash against the entire physical point stock, not merely accessible stock; reserved point energy cannot be counted again as cargo for another deposit or loss. Existing physical household contracts pass this tightened conservation rule.

Unpayable passive-mode expenses are refused before mutation. Even payable static expenses with an unspecified physical location are refused: withholding scalar budget without knowing which points were consumed cannot certify post-intent passive eligibility or exact native charge order. Future addressed expense/workforce schedules must supply that information. Static non-passive behavior remains available. Refills remain explicit addressed transfers; no synthetic future cash is added.
