## Bounded pathable-room graph

capabilities/intel/room-graph snapshots injected cardinal exits per request, including unknown results, and filters blocked intermediates/destinations through an injected admission policy. It adds a diagonal strategic neighbor only when at least one real permitted origin→orthogonal→diagonal path exists. The edge retains its via witness. No Game/Memory or feature imports occur here.

room-gap runs bounded breadth-first search. It keeps the legacy strategic metric where that witnessed corner counts as one gap, while returning every real cardinal room hop in route evidence. This number is not physical room-crossing count, and diagonal edges are not direct movement commands. Actual creep movement remains the movement service's responsibility.

Depth/read caps and admission are explicit; a read cap counts unique exit-map reads, including orthogonal corner witnesses and unknown reads. Unknown graph, depth/read exhaustion, admission refusal, blocked destination and proven unreachable have distinct outcomes. A found route after incomplete exploration is explicitly exact=false: it is an upper bound, not a certified minimum. Caller policy must not promote incomplete results to safe reachability or shortest-distance claims.

There is no symmetric result cache. Directed/excluded/changed graphs are recomputed on a new request. Inputs must be stable snapshots for the request; cached arrays are detached from caller mutation. Canonical world room names and cardinal exit topology are validated instead of silently accepting impossible jumps. Generic geometry does not decide hostility, stale-intel risk or room-status admission; future feature policy owns those decisions.

## Automatic visible-room capture

A single empire-scoped runtime job consumes existing visible-room snapshots and effective room policies. Shared capture CPU, scan and attempt limits are environment/empire settings; profile/room/temporary overrides of those limits fail validation rather than silently multiplying or ignoring the budget. Each room still owns enablement, interval, freshness threshold and its normal background/bucket/CPU admission. Runtime room jobs retain their owned/visible scope; empire jobs run once per tick independently of owned-room count.

The feature owns intel-capture/empire, a validated revision/cursor record. Canonical visible room names are deduplicated and sorted; each batch begins after the last scanned cursor and wraps. A disappeared room is not required to advance. Failed or newly hidden capture attempts consume the shared cap, advance the cursor and retain prior facts. A metadata scan counts against its separate cap; disabled rooms do not read intel. Guards run before scans and after metadata, so a deferred pending capture remains next rather than being falsely acknowledged. Progress uses revision comparison; unchanged cursors avoid writes. Capturing a whole room, migrating/validating snapshots and saving progress are cooperative atomic costs, so the CPU allowance is admission control rather than a hard elapsed ceiling.

Unknown/stale detail or the regular interval makes a visible room eligible. Recent capture failures back off for that room's interval; route/scout failures cannot suppress refresh. Exceptions are room-qualified, preserve authoritative facts, and do not prevent later rooms from running even if the reporting sink fails. Automatic capture does not mark a physical visit. Zero budgets or all-disabled capture perform no native/state work; unknown lookup remains read-only. This creates no vision, scout demand or observer action, and does not authorize traversal through stale/hostile rooms.

## Freshness-aware traversal admission

Traversal separates request-owned assessment/cache/budgets, input validation, threat classification and knowledge/cost decisions. The factory validates canonical rooms and detached room-bound facts at the injected boundary, uses an explicit self/allies policy and reads current map status separately from historical observations. A new request is required for changed diplomacy, ownership, room status or observations; returned cached decisions cannot be mutated. Assessment attempts, including failed fact reads, consume the request budget. Guard and room-budget refusal expose incomplete diagnostics.

Known-safe mode rejects unknown or stale detail and asks for refresh. Exploration can admit unknown/stale rooms at explicit costs with confidence=uncertain; it never certifies them safe. Stale known dangers remain blocked until a new authoritative observation clears them. Controller ownership/reservation, hostile creeps/power units and dangerous hostile tower/invader-core facts exclude a room. Detailed allied identities and passive power-bank facts refine raw hostile indexes; legacy/unrecognized hostile IDs remain conservatively unclassified and require refresh. Keeper admission/penalty is explicit. Blocked rooms, temporary avoids and non-normal/unknown current map status apply to destinations as well as intermediates.

pathableIntelRoomGap composes the policy with the existing bounded real-corner graph. Returned confidence/refreshRooms describe entered-room intel; a zero-hop route enters no room. Strategic gap stays the legacy unweighted corner metric, independently of the policy's travel costs. Both topology-read and intel-assessment limits retain uncertainty: an unavailable result caused by policy exhaustion says room-budget/admission, while any found partial route remains exact=false. A pruned unknown policy read cannot become certified unreachable or a shortest-path claim. Callers using the raw allows predicate must preserve its incomplete diagnostics; the feature-level gap helper does so.

Porting review: role.scout.js495–548/remote.manager.js142–168 gave start/target rooms an unconditional route exemption. The new admission policy deliberately removes the target exemption; escaping a dangerous origin is a separate lifecycle action rather than permission to re-enter that destination. Legacy scout defaults include stabilization1tick, danger radius5, temporary hostile avoidance1500, route-failure avoidance1000, stale detail5000, local/extended exploration depths2/4; repeated-death route danger uses2losses in5000 while expansion scoring uses a10000window. These remain source references for the unfinished scout/death policies, not claims that they are already implemented. Existing movement progress, traffic and passability must be reused; in particular private foreign ramparts cannot be treated walkable as the legacy scout matrix did.
