# Native initial-cohort proposal strategy

ED-29 now derives a finite miner/courier project from a current complete native travel quote and explicit cohort policy. The quote supplies exact ordered bodies, selected spawn/exit, station, collection/deposit tiles, measured approach/return legs, native source/stock/endpoint/spawn facts and assignment generation. The policy supplies horizon, observation validity, protected energy, retirement margin, batch, priorities, courier readiness, repair policy and explicit assignment-renewal assumption. The result flows directly into the cooperative candidate evaluator.

This strategy creates one initial miner and one courier. It defers observed incumbent labor rather than pairing it with an overlapping speculative miner. Existing lower-level projection/import strategies remain available for paid-incumbent cohorts. Quoted return travel plus deposit and margin determines retirement; an approach that exhausts service life or a batch larger than the quoted courier's capacity is refused. Optional courier readiness permits distinct eager/delayed finite alternatives. It does not model adaptive readiness tied to observed construction completion; that requires a separate strategy.

The existing native converter handles current facts, reservation transitions, queued competition, actual spawn availability, physical funding/claims, source stock/phase, remaining construction, initial station stock, endpoint capacity and durability. No refill, outside income, future replacement, reservation renewal or sink-release schedule is invented. Birth IDs/names in this project are counterfactual model identities. They are not newly reserved production identities and must never be blindly enqueued as real work. Actual producers retain identity allocation, ownership and final admission.

Candidate validity cannot exceed the current assignment expiry or configured observation window. If the forecast extends to/beyond assignment expiry, assumeAssignmentRenewal must explicitly be true; otherwise authority-horizon defers it. This is an ongoing-assignment counterfactual, not an issued renewal or guarantee of future authority. Known physical reservation expiry remains refused by the native converter. Withdrawal, hostile pressure, changed generation or world facts still invalidate future real admission. The final real-effect gate must remain read-only and current.

The producer snapshots quote/policy before callbacks, performs read-only projection and binds full quote/policy/strategy identity to the candidate. It creates no new namespace, queue, lease or resource claim. Default runtime configuration/adoption, automatic comparable geometry/body alternatives, operational same-strategy schedules, broader native economic/lifecycle calibration and multi-room capital stability remain open. This initial-cohort strategy does not replace those requirements.
