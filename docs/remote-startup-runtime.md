## Configuration and composition

`remoteStartupPolicy` defaults to `eager`, preserving existing recruitment behavior; `staged` activates the new rules per home through ordinary configuration precedence. `remoteStartupEnergyReserve` defaults to 0 and is bounded 0..1,000,000. `remoteStartupCourierLead` defaults to 0, bounded 0..1,500 extra ticks in addition to quoted travel and native body spawn time. The total projected interval is capped to the current miner's available service and 1,500 ticks.

Main creates one startup service with injected state, runtime settings and the existing native structure-energy admission read port. Its producer permit is injected into source, reservation and construction recruitment. Its final gate runs before native spawning and independently rechecks current facts and funding. Eager/unrelated paths do not open new state or read native startup observations. No startup state namespace, competing stock owner, movement owner or second energy-reservation ledger is introduced.

## Infrastructure and staging

The native survey caches only the current tick's source/container/site observation. A visible energy source with an adjacent container is ready; an owned adjacent container site is building; no container/site is missing; absent vision or non-energy targets are unknown. Unknown facts do not appear as completed or free collection infrastructure. Sources can launch a bootstrap miner when container construction is enabled, while ordinary fleet admission still validates current lease, source profile, safe target and route. This permits a pioneer to restore vision rather than requiring an already present remote worker to start one.

Couriers require an actual owned source miner. A ready container permits birth; otherwise the bounded construction forecast must finish within spawn/travel/extra lead using a healthy, unboosted miner currently standing on the site's station tile. Source power effects, unknown facts, insufficient remaining worker service and unsupported positions hold new haulers. Other workers may complete the site sooner; this projection deliberately does not invent their labor. Reserver births wait for every source in the current room lease to have both a container and a source miner. Existing actor service remains owned by its original task/authority logic.

Paid in-flight births remain selected by the producer rather than being cancelled merely because their already spent cost lowered cash. Base lease/configuration/safety cancellation remains in force. Settled source cohorts need no speculative funding check for a nonexistent extra birth. Final admission is stricter: only genuinely due source births can pass, and every additional birth still rechecks phase and current capital. A stale or manually reinserted request cannot exploit producer preservation of a paid cohort.

## Funding and ownership

Spendable home stock is the shared structure-admission remainder for spawn/extension energy plus storage and terminal energy, reduced by existing active energy-stock quantity claims for those pools. In-transit cargo and projected receipts are not banked cash. Claims are read through validated detached state without opening/replacing their ledger. Transfers within the home can temporarily reduce this conservative cash view until their normal receipt/claim settlement occurs.

A source with a live/spawning miner or courier, or a proposed new miner, creates a read-only liability for all currently needed quoted source workers. The shared population reconciler supplies due miner and courier counts, including replacement timing and healthy/spawning cohorts. Already queued unpaid bodies remain a liability; already born cohorts are counted once. The current proposed body is removed from that liability and charged once separately. A new source cannot consume the missing crew funding of an existing one; reserver and construction births also respect that commitment. This protects quoted complete crews, not an assumed future revenue stream or perpetual worker supply.

The finite cash forecast evaluates the proposed birth and outstanding crew liability at a zero-tick horizon with the configured protected reserve. Native same-tick energy claims reduce subsequent admissions, so two accepted births cannot spend the same journaled spawn energy. Self-harvested container construction is not charged to the home bank. Other owners still govern actual construction funding, logistics stock, local emergency spending and accepted native effects; this policy specifically constrains remote births rather than guaranteeing a global bank minimum against every possible expense.
