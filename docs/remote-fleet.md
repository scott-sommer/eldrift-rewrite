# Automatic remote workforce

ED-29 remains in progress. The normal game loop now connects committed automatic portfolios to measured-body workforce publication, recruitment, miner/courier execution, confirmed banking and retirement. Automatic Reserve pricing/execution, main-loop infrastructure orchestration, adaptive support capacity, full-life unreachable feedback, cross-home/home-loss qualification and specialist/operator integration remain open. The scenarios use an existing remote container; they do not establish automatic road/container building.

## Ownership and composition

A fleet owner encodes home, remote room, source and actor kind. Lease renewal preserves that population identity; generation stays in the profile/task authority. Only a successful scoped management commit publishes its quoted miner/courier bodies, travel times and repriced hauler requirement. Publication preserves retired scopes for cleanup, with bounded schemas and capacity checks. Portfolio selection now constrains total source/support slots plus haulers before committing leases, rather than discovering a worker-cap violation after publication. Unoccupied retired scopes are pruned.

Recruitment reuses applyPopulationDemand and the shared spawn queue. Replacement timing includes the measured travel, spawn time and margin. Damaged/missing planned body capacity cannot suppress a replacement; retiring old workers keep cargo. Changed queued bodies are cancelled under their exact owner. Final dispatch separately checks current flags/body/target/lease authority before invoking spawn, so a bounded recruitment scan cannot leave unsafe queued work executable. Unrelated queue owners remain untouched.

Already-spawning cancellation goes through the existing durable structure/body admission owner. It requires the exact current spawning name, actor owner/home, owned spawn and owned spawning creep. Rejection or interruption retains admission for the tick. Cancellation does not speculate about immediate disappearance or refund energy. The pinned engine removes the spawning creep and clears the spawn without refunding its spent energy.

## Execution and accounting

Owned-room recruitment and empire actor execution are separate. The empire loop can still withdraw a worker when its original home is absent from the owned-room feature list. Per-home and empire cursors preserve fairness across resets; each home retains its worker-service allowance. Actors use the existing remote mining and hauling/banking cycles, action arbiter, movement dispatch, quantity pools and journals. Main creep maintenance includes remote task definitions. Withdrawal now explicitly cancels an old remote mining task even when its underlying lease is still valid; it cannot silently resume work after policy withdrawal.

Prior-tick transfer liabilities are settled before optional bounded actor work. A default-budget development run exposed that rotating actors could otherwise miss the confirmation tick and conservatively classify ordinary deliveries as uncertain. The receipt barrier fixes that scheduling error rather than fabricating credit or widening the action allowance. Mandatory settlement/roster maintenance is distinct from optional worker-service CPU. Production aggregate CPU/Memory and pressure qualification remain ED-43 work.

Retirement eligibility includes the measured trip/travel margin. Loaded workers return through shared home banking, with no income credited on issuance. Unsafe/expired/withdrawn assignments stop service; a missing safe home preserves cargo while the later home-loss/refuge policy remains pending. Zero source/worker capacity or disabled work stops new work; disabling spawning alone preserves service by existing workers while cancelling queued/spawning demand.

## Controls

remoteManagementEnabled owns discovery/selection; remoteWorkersEnabled activates published workforce service; remoteSpawningEnabled independently permits recruitment. All default off. remoteWorkerCpuBudget defaults to five milliseconds for optional worker service; remoteWorkerMaximumUnits bounds productive workforce; remoteMinimumBatch, remoteTripLifetime and remoteSpawnRequestLifetime configure logistics/recruitment cadence. Existing flags for room, path, source, hauler and economic budgets remain in src/config/remotes.ts. The native ordinary policy currently requests no reservation or keeper/mineral workforce.

## Outstanding model/handoff audit

The finite-CARRY duty-cycle correction is implemented at 068049b. Shared harvestCycleRate models C / (ceil(C / P) + 1) for carry capacity C and harvest power P; zero-CARRY direct harvesting retains rate P. remoteProduction caps that rate against source regeneration before sizing transport and calculating net yield. For example, 50 carry capacity and eight harvest power require seven harvesting ticks plus a handoff, giving 6.25 nominal station throughput when source availability permits. Tests drive the actual miner state machine through finite-capacity native-style effects, covering partial last harvests, different capacities/powers and zero-CARRY collection. Marginal profitability rejection and required hauling change with the corrected rate. Station sink availability is assumed; building, repairs, interruptions and direct-drop collection losses require separate costs/qualification. Short unreserved/burst probes do not prove reserved/high-throughput service.

Retirement-aware recruitment now includes spawn time, outbound travel, the existing service retirement window and a scheduling buffer. One natural-lifetime wave is qualified below. Pricing still needs to use the actual replacement interval, and loaded miner return requires its own measured bound; the outbound miner quote is not a general loaded-return proof. Keep mandatory receipt settlement outside optional action scheduling, and measure its aggregate cost rather than interpreting a five-millisecond action allowance as a total feature CPU guarantee.
